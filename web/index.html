PATIENT EXPERIENCE

Preparing for Your Visit

We strive to keep consultations calm, thorough, and on-time. Here is what to keep in mind before you arrive.

Average wait time: Under 8 minutes
01

What to Bring

Please bring a government photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of all current medications or supplements you take.

02

Arrive 10 Minutes Early

Quick digital check-in ensures your vitals are updated without delaying your time with your doctor.

03

Prescription Refills

Routine refills are best handled during your consultation. You can also ask our AI assistant to queue refill requests.

04

Cancellation Notice

If your schedule changes, kindly provide 24 hours notice so our team can offer the appointment to another patient in need.

No referral required for primary care
