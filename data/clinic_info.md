# WellCare

Type: family and primary care.

## Hours
Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 8 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

## Location & Contact
Address: Banaglore. Phone: 674-252-024.

## Services
- General check-ups: routine visits and health reviews.
- Vaccinations: seasonal and travel vaccines.
- Ongoing care: follow-ups for long-term conditions.

## Preparing for your visit
- Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of current medications.
- Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in forms.
- Write down your questions beforehand.

## Appointments
Requests are sent to the front desk, which contacts the patient to confirm the time. Maximum 8 appointments per day.

## Emergencies
Call 8785415621; do not use this chat.

## What the assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot diagnose, advise on treatment or medication, or interpret test results.
